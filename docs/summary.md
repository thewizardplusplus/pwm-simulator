# Summary (EN / [RU](summary_ru.md))

[<< Back](README.md)

![](screenshot.png)

The **blue** plot displays a random analog signal. The **light green** plot displays the signal derived from the digital signal (see below) by passing it through an [RC circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RC_circuit).

The digital signal is generated by the user: holding the left mouse button or touching the right half of the screen sets it to **logical high**. Otherwise, it is **logical low**. Holding the right mouse button or touching the left half of the screen makes the light green plot decrease twice as fast, even if the upward control is also held.

The goal of the game is to replicate the shape of the blue plot by the light green plot as close as possible.
